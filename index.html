About The Studio

Why "11:11"?

You know the moment — you glance at the clock, it reads 11:11, and for half a second you make a wish. That's the studio's whole premise: someone has an idea worth wishing for, and we happen to own the printers to make it real.

What started as printing parts and favors for friends turned into custom figures, business signage, cosplay armor, and keepsakes people actually cry over. Every job is modeled, sliced, and finished in-house — no outsourcing, no templates pretending to be "custom."

01

Send the idea

A photo, a sketch, a screenshot, a link — whatever gets the concept across.

02

We model & quote

We design or source the model, pick the right material, and send a firm price.

03

Print & finish

Printed layer by layer, then sanded, glued, or painted depending on the piece.

04

Ship or pickup

Packed to survive the trip, with local pickup available on request.

PLA

Detail & Display

Our go-to for figures, signage, and anything that needs crisp detail and clean color. Best fit for indoor décor and gifts.

PETG

Strength & Durability

Tougher and more flexible — used for functional parts, outdoor pieces, and anything that'll take a knock.

ABS

Heat & Impact Resistance

Our pick for props, automotive-adjacent parts, and builds that need to survive heat or hard use.
